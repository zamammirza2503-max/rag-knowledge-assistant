# Company Handbook

## Vacation

Full-time employees receive 28 paid vacation days per calendar year. Vacation should normally be requested at least five working days in advance.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Teams can define additional in-office days when collaboration requires it.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and standard accessories. Employees should report damaged equipment to IT within two business days. Normal wear is covered by the company. Damage caused by negligence may require an employee contribution after an IT review.

## Learning Budget

Employees receive an annual professional development budget of 800 EUR for approved courses, conferences, and technical books.
